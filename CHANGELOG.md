# Double Holy Cards for Tainted Lost changelog

## Unreleased

### Added

- For Tainted Lost, every randomly rolled card gets an extra roll to become a Holy Card, raising the chance from
  vanilla's 10% to 20%. Applies to room drops, chests, shops (the card stays paid) and items that roll cards, such as
  Deck of Cards.
- Cards dropped from the inventory, scripted cards with a fixed subtype, runes, soul stones and special objects (Dice
  Shard, Cracked Key, ...) stay as they are. Other characters are not affected.
